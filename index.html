01 · Where It Started

One Hiring Conversation Became a Full Transformation Agenda

The engagement began in September 2025 with a single brief: help Dinshaw’s find its first CHRO. By the first plant visit 6 weeks later, the promoters and STAIR had agreed a far wider agenda.

The Starting Point

A promoter-led, ~₹1,000 Cr business with strong roots in Central India across ice cream, dairy, bakery and namkeen, ready to move from a family-run model to a professionally managed company.

The first STAIR deliverable was a CHRO job description and skill matrix, scored with the promoters’ own weightages.

The Agenda Set on 31 October 2025

STAIR’s first visit to the Nagpur office and plant set the 5 areas that still frame the work.

  1. HR and leadership hiring
  2. Sales enablement
  3. Data, reporting and ERP
  4. Quick commerce and e-commerce
  5. Finance and operations analytics
02 · The Journey

5 Phases, Each One a Step Up

  1. Phase 1 · Sep to Dec 2025

    Foundation

    Setting the hiring standard, choosing search partners and resetting how marketing money is spent.

    1. Sep 2025First CHRO discussion with the promoters; JD and scored skill matrix created
    2. 23 Sep 2025SalesCode.ai assessed as a tool for retailer-facing sales teams
    3. 14 Oct 20254 executive search firms introduced: Gilbert Tweed, Executive Access, Questa and EMA Partners
    4. 31 Oct 2025First Nagpur visit to the office and plant; 5-area agenda agreed
    5. 15 Nov 2025EMA Partners appointed, on terms strengthened by STAIR
    6. 20 Nov 2025First SAP integrator meeting; ERP journey begins
    7. 5 Dec 2025Marketing Spends Gold Standard: a ~₹15 Cr plan with a channel-by-channel split
    8. 15 Dec 2025Second Nagpur visit: quality circles and IoT work begin; agency timeline set
    9. Dec 20256 marketing agencies pitch; Values Manual for incoming leaders drafted
    Key Decision

    Marketing moves to performance-led digital (10 Dec), and the 2 CEO roles become Business Heads for Dairy and Ice Cream (15 Dec).

  2. Phase 2 · Jan to Apr 2026

    Design

    Choosing the ERP, shaping the organisation and launching the season’s campaigns.

    1. 4 Mar 2026PR for the Dildaar campaign re-scoped, reducing the fee from ~₹30 lakh to ~₹7 lakh
    2. 13 Mar 2026SAP Implementation Scope of Work and ERP Blueprint delivered
    3. Mar 2026Nagpur reviews on hiring, factory, financials, forecasting, sales automation and SAP; factory AI vendors met
    4. 11 Apr 2026BEI Confluence onboarded as the marketing agency
    5. 13 Apr 2026ERP evaluation, organisation structure and Milky Mist case study presented
    6. 20 Apr 2026Cold-room energy pilot approved, with a path to all 60 cold rooms at Butibori
    7. 28 Apr 20263x Chocolate Cone campaign and TVC released
    8. 30 Apr 2026Nagpur visit: SAP S/4HANA Public Cloud with a clean-core design agreed
    Key Decision

    SAP chosen over QAD, and a 2-tier leadership model adopted with Business Heads under the promoters (13 Apr).

  3. Phase 3 · May to Jul 2026

    Build

    The first leaders sign on, quick commerce takes off and the SAP programme gets its partner.

    1. 7 May 2026Cumulative quick commerce revenue crosses ₹1 Cr; SAP task force formed
    2. 19 May 2026Business Head mandates for Ice Cream and Dairy signed
    3. 2 Jun 2026Low-cost AI vision on existing CCTV chosen over heavy IoT hardware
    4. 22 Jun 2026SAP gap analysis and solution design workshops
    5. 1–4 Jul 2026Nagpur Strategic Review: hiring, ERP, factory AI, Base and Aspiration Plans, peer benchmarking
    6. 6 Jul 2026Q1 FY 2026-27 progress report issued
    7. 9 Jul 2026Business Head Dairy accepts the appointment
    8. 17 Jul 2026Plant energy audit reviewed: ₹2.38 Cr a year of savings identified
    9. 28 Jul 2026SAP governance pack submitted; Executive Access agreement closed
    Key Decision

    GFI preferred as SAP partner: 7-month implementation plus 12 months of support, with AI added after ERP stabilises (4 Jul).

  4. Phase 4 · Aug to Sep 2026

    Scale

    Focusing on one market, setting factory priorities and building the governance model.

    1. 1 Aug 2026Pay philosophy agreed: at least 65% fixed, performance variable and 36-month retention
    2. 12 Aug 2026Brand Manager joins
    3. 12–13 Aug 2026Nagpur visit: Ice Cream and Dairy confirmed as the 2 lead verticals; 4 factory priorities set
    4. 21 Aug 2026Project Pune named the quick commerce priority; RFID discovery with ID Tech
    5. 28 Aug 2026Business Head Ice Cream signs
    6. 31 Aug 2026HRMS demos by ZingHR and BlueTree in Nagpur
    7. 5 Sep 2026Dairy quick commerce campaign goes live (Ghee, Paneer, Curd, Milk)
    8. Sep 2026Spain and Malta shelf research; Promoter-Professional Engagement Framework and Board Structure note
    9. 21 Sep 2026PMG Engineers order received for a 3-year plant upgrade; skyr chosen as the R&D focus
    Key Decision

    Win Pune first: platform advertising over discounting, with new cities opened only once Pune performs (21 Aug).

  5. Phase 5 · October 2026

    Now

    New products, a reset with Instamart, and the leadership team close to complete.

    1. 30 Sep–1 Oct 2026Nagpur review: 32 action items; Base Plan signed off; separate P&Ls by vertical; store design chosen
    2. 1 Oct 2026New product note delivered: skyr and an unflavoured probiotic dahi
    3. 3 Oct 2026Meeting with Ms Nandita Sinha, CEO of Instamart
    4. 5 Oct 2026Sales Head Namkeen & Bakery finalised
    5. 8 Oct 2026CHRO final offer issued; Head IT interviews begin
    6. 9 Oct 2026Q2 progress report issued; HRMS pilot secured from both vendors at no cost
    Key Decision

    Skyr over Greek yogurt: high protein, low fat, no added sugar, sold through quick commerce and corporate channels (30 Sep).
